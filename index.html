About The Platform

Empowering Student Clubs & Event Organizers

Organizing college hackathons, technical workshops, cultural fests, and campus seminars takes tremendous effort. Writing the same event details over and over for Instagram, WhatsApp groups, official notices, and emails is time-consuming and prone to errors.

Why Event Mate?

Saves Hours of Work

Type your venue, date, and description once. Instant copy-ready kits generated in under 1 second.

Multilingual Inclusivity

Native support for English, Telugu (తెలుగు), and Hindi (हिंदी) so regional campus communities are included.

100% Free & No Vendor Lock-in

Runs locally in any browser or with standard Firebase. No expensive subscription fees or forced software.

Complete Privacy & Transparency

No secret background spamming. Review messages before sending with official WhatsApp click-to-chat & mailto drafts.

Built for Hackathon Presentations

Event Mate is engineered with clean, modular HTML5, modern CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript. It requires zero compilation, zero Node.js build steps, and can be demonstrated on any laptop simply by opening index.html.

Help Center

How to Use Event Mate

Follow these simple steps to generate and distribute your event communications.

1

Enter Event Details

Provide the event title, category, date, venue, description, and registration link in the Create Event form.

2

Generate All Content

Click "Generate All Content" to instantly produce captions, official notices, WhatsApp messages, reminders, and FAQs.

3

Edit & Copy

Customize any message directly using the inline "Edit" button, or copy any card to your clipboard with one click.

4

Save & Reuse

Your events are preserved in your browser's local storage and can be reopened or downloaded anytime from "My Events".

5

Share Instantly

Click "Open in WhatsApp" to preview in chat, or "Create Email Draft" to load pre-filled subjects and bodies in your mail app.
